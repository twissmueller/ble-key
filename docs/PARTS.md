# Parts to buy

Bought parts for the BLE Key add-ons and for the IC-705 under-shelf mount, per unit built, with the
Reichelt article where Reichelt has one. Prices and stock as seen on reichelt.de on 2026-11-14; all
listed Reichelt articles were in stock. "Buy" is what to put in the basket for one unit plus a
spare, given the pack sizes.

Printed parts are not listed here; they come from the scripts (`enclosure/addons.py` here,
`mount.py` and `mount_swivel.py` in `~/projects/prints/ic705-shelf-mount`).

## IC-705 under-shelf mount

Reichelt carries only the plain M6 and M8 nuts, washers and bolts of this list, and those only in
packs of 100. The thumbscrew, the star knobs and the wood screws in the right length are not in
Reichelt's range; for those the Amazon sources from `ic705-shelf-mount/shop/NOTES.md` stay
(checked there on 2026-11-10).

For single mounts the M6/M8 set is the cheaper way: it covers the M8 bolt and both washers, and its
M6 × 40 bolts are the pedal's hinge bolts too; the self-locking M6 nuts for the tilt axis and the
hinges come from Reichelt (`SKSM M6-100`). Reichelt's packs of 100
pay off only for a series.
