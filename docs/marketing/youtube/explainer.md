# BLE-Key: Make any Morse key wireless (about 2 min)

**Title:** Turn your Morse key or paddle into a Bluetooth key: open hardware with a XIAO board
**Description:** BLE-Key reads your straight key or paddle through a 3.5 mm jack and streams the keying over Bluetooth LE to Longpath on phone, tablet or Mac. Open firmware for the XIAO ESP32-S3 and nRF52840; build it yourself or get a kit.
Shop: https://shop.ramuslabs.com/ble-key/ · Longpath: https://ramuslabs.com/longpath/ · GitHub: https://github.com/twissmueller/ble-key
**Chapters:** 0:00 The idea · 0:20 Inside · 0:50 Connecting · 1:20 Practising · 1:45 Build or buy

## Scene 1: The idea (on camera, key + board on the desk)
- I wanted to practise on my own paddle, without a radio and without a cable to the laptop.
- So I put a tiny Bluetooth board between the paddle and the app.

## Scene 2: Inside (close-ups)
- It's a Seeed XIAO, ESP32-S3 or nRF52840. *(board)*
- The paddle goes into the 3.5 mm jack: tip is dit, ring is dah.
- The key makes no sound itself. It just sends exact keying edges. The LED shows when you key.

## Scene 3: Connecting (screen, Longpath)
- USB-C for power, then in Longpath it shows up as "Paddle". Tap, connected.

## Scene 4: Practising (on camera + screen)
- Now the app makes the sidetone, decodes what I send and shows my timing. *(send CQ)*
- Works with straight keys, single and iambic paddles.

## Scene 5: Build or buy (on camera)
- Firmware and enclosure are open on GitHub. Build your own.
- Or get a parts kit or an assembled key from the shop. Links below. 73.
